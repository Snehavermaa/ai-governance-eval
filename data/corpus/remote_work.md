# Remote Work Policy

After completing probation, employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. The probation period lasts 6 months.

Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a Vice President. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours from 11 am to 4 pm IST.

Each employee receives a one-time home office allowance of 15000 rupees. The allowance can be used for a chair, desk or monitor.
