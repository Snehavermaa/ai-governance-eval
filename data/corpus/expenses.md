# Travel and Expense Policy

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Original receipts are required for any single expense above 500 rupees.

The hotel limit for domestic travel is 6000 rupees per night. The hotel limit for international travel is 180 US dollars per night.

Economy class must be booked for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights longer than 8 hours with approval from a Director.

Approved claims are reimbursed within 15 working days. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.
